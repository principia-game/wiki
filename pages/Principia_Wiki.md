The Principia Wiki is a central resource of knowledge all about Principia and related topics. It has seen multiple iterations over the years, with the current iteration being hosted on principia-web.se and maintained by ROllerozxa.

## First iteration
The first iteration of the Principia Wiki was available at `wiki.principiagame.com`, launched by Bithack around the release of the game. It ran MediaWiki with the standard Vector theme and was open to anyone with a Bithack account to edit.

The first iteration of the wiki went down along with the rest of the Principia community site in early 2018. In 2020, ROllerozxa had reconstructed [a full archive of the old wiki contents](https://github.com/principia-historical/wiki-archive) in Markdown format from a MediaWiki content dump.

## Second iteration
The second iteration of the Principia Wiki was launched in September 2022, hosted on `principia-web.se` by ROllerozxa. It runs a custom wiki software specifically for the Principia community site, and uses Markdown for page formatting instead of MediaWiki's wikitext.

After the wiki's launch, the wiki was initially populated with pages for every object and item from the [Custom-Object-Help](https://github.com/principia-historical/custom-object-help) project, which was originally started by ROllerozxa to expand the game's built-in object and item help texts prior to the open source release. Then all content from the Markdown archive of the first iteration of the wiki was imported, merging together all object pages in the process. The result was a superset of all previously written knowledge about Principia in one central place, which was continued to be expanded upon by ROllerozxa.

In September 2023, the Principia Wiki's content was migrated into a Git repository [principia-game/wiki](https://github.com/principia-game/wiki) to ease the maintenance and allow the wiki's content to be easily downloaded in full. The previous wiki-style editing was replaced with GitHub pull requests to prevent vandalism.
