Keith Tripp was a Principia community member going by the username of zardOz. He was well-known for his contributions in helping new players to Principia, and made a lot of edits to the old Principia Wiki.

Keith passed away on the 18th August 2015, but the community were not made aware of his passing until many months later. The level [R.I.P zardOz](https://principia-web.se/archive/level/20830) was uploaded by Golden on the 15th February 2016 as a tribute to the old community site archive.

## Dedication
The Principia source repository contains a dedication to Keith in the [`THANKS.md`](https://github.com/Bithack/principia/blob/master/THANKS.md) file, from the original author of Principia:

> **In Memory of Keith Tripp**
>
> I would like to dedicate a few lines to Keith Tripp, aka zardOz. By definition the go-to guy when you had a Principia question, Keith helped newcomers understand and get started building things. Keith created hundreds of tutorial levels and was always one of the first to learn the latest object. Unfortunately, I was notified of his passing late 2015. There will always be tutorials, but never another Keith Tripp.
>
> I was told Principia inspired Keith to become interested in programming. He would have loved to see Principia being open sourced.
>
> Emil
