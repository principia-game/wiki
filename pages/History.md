This page aims to serve as a brief, non-exhaustive timeline over major events for Principia and its predecessor Apparatus, giving a rough timeline of the game's history.

## Apparatus era
- **May 2010** - [[Bithack]] is founded as a Swedish company
- **March 2011** - [[Tesla Plushies]] is released for Android
- **May 2011** - First public [Apparatus](https://apparatus.voxelmanip.se) release for Android
- **August 2011** - [Apparatus 1.0 releases](https://apparatus.voxelmanip.se/archive/apparatus-is-out-of-beta/)
- **December 2011** - Final proper Apparatus update released (1.1 Christmas Update)
- **July 2012** - Principia is announced with a level on the Apparatus community site (["Something BIG is coming..."](https://web.archive.org/web/20131010195738/http://www.apparatusgame.com/level.php?id=17411))
- **August 2012** - The Bithack forums are launched
- **Sometime 2013** - The Apparatus community site gets defaced due to an XSS vulnerability in the comments section

## Principia - Proprietary era
- **September 2013** - Well-known Apparatus players are given access to the Principia closed beta
- **November 2013** - Principia is released for Android
- **November 2013** - Apparatus 1.2 is released, breaking all levels
- **December 2013** - Principia 1.3 - Christmas Update is released
- **January 2014** - Final Apparatus release (1.2.1)
- **February 2014** - Principia 1.4 is released, and the game is made available for Windows and iOS
- **August 2014** - Principia 1.5 is released
- **September 2014** - Indiegogo fundraiser to fund further development is launched, finishes at the end of the month having only raised a fraction of the goal
- **October 2014** - Last commercial version 1.5.1 released, Bithack goes silent as the game's development abruptly ends
- **August 2015** - [[zardOz]] passes away (the community hears about it sometime later)
- **February 2016** - Bithack's web infrastructure is migrated to a new server
- **April 2016** - First announcement of [releasing Principia as open source](Principia_Open_Source_Project), but plans fell through at the time
- **February-March 2018** - Principia and Apparatus community sites as hosted by Bithack are permanently shut down
- **April 2019** - [[Apparatus 2019]] kickstarter is revealed, but ended up being unsuccessful
- **December 2020** - ROllerozxa starts hosting principia-web as an unofficial revival site
- **March 2022** - principia-web moves to the current principia-web.se domain name

## Principia - Open Source era
- **August 2022** - The community gets back in touch with sdac and the [Principia source code is released to be developed by the community](https://principia-web.se/news/6)
- **September 2022** - The new [[Principia Wiki]] is launched on principia-web.se
- **December 2022** - ROllerozxa becomes project maintainer as sdac steps away
- **December 2022** - [Principia 2022.12.31 is released](https://principia-web.se/news/8) as the first open source release (labelled a beta build of Principia 1.5.2 at the time)
- **February 2024** - [Principia 2024.02.29](https://principia-web.se/news/13) is released, being the first version with the new calendar-based versioning scheme
- **May 2024** - Principia for Android [is released on F-Droid](https://principia-web.se/news/14)
- **August 2024** - The first building contest in about a decade is held, [Destruction Frenzy](https://principia-web.se/contest/1)
- **December 2025** - Principia [now runs in the web browser](https://principia-web.se/news/22)
- **August 2026** - [Four year anniversary](https://principia-web.se/news/32) of Principia's open source release
- **August 2026** - [Principia 2026.08.16](https://principia-web.se/news/33) is released, finally replacing the GTK-based dialogs with new Dear Imgui-based dialogs
