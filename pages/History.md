This page aims to serve as a brief, non-exhaustive timeline over major events for Principia and its predecessor Apparatus, giving a rough timeline of the game's history.

## Apparatus era
- May 2011 - First public Apparatus release
- August 2011 - [Apparatus 1.0 releases](https://apparatus.voxelmanip.se/archive/apparatus-is-out-of-beta/)
- December 2011 - Final proper Apparatus update released (1.1 Christmas Update)
- July 2012 - Principia is announced with a level on the Apparatus community site (["Something BIG is coming..."](https://web.archive.org/web/20131010195738/http://www.apparatusgame.com/level.php?id=17411))
- August 2012 - The Bithack forums are launched
- Sometime 2013 - The Apparatus community site gets defaced due to an XSS vulnerability in the comments section

## Principia - Proprietary era
- September 2013 - Well-known Apparatus players are given access to the Principia closed beta
- November 2013 - Principia is released for Android
- November 2013 - Apparatus 1.2 is released breaking all levels
- January 2014 - Final Apparatus release (1.2.1)
- February 2014 - Principia is released for Windows and iOS
- August 2014 - Principia 1.5 is released
- September 2014 - Indiegogo fundraiser to fund further development is launched, finishes at the end of the month having only raised a fraction of the goal
- October 2014 - Last commercial version 1.5.1 released, Bithack goes silent
- August 2015 - [[zardOz]] passes away (the community hears about it sometime later)
- April 2016 - First announcement of [releasing Principia as open source](Principia_Open_Source_Project), but never ended up happening
- February-March 2018 - Principia and Apparatus community sites are shut down
- April 2019 - [[Apparatus 2019]] kickstarter is revealed, but ended up being unsuccessful
- December 2020 - ROllerozxa starts hosting principia-web as an unofficial revival site

## Principia - Open Source era
- August 2022 - We get back in touch with sdac and the [Principia source code is released to be developed by the community](https://principia-web.se/news/6)
- September 2022 - The new [Principia Wiki](https://principia-web.se/wiki/)
- December 2022 - ROllerozxa becomes project maintainer as sdac steps away
- May 2024 - Principia for Android [is released on F-Droid](https://principia-web.se/news/14)
